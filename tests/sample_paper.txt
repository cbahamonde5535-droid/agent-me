Trauma-Informed Care in Schools

Trauma affects many students (Smith and Jones, 2020). Research shows outcomes improve (Lee et al., 2019; Garcia, 2021). Brown, Davis, and Wilson (2018) found mixed results. Adams & Clark (2017) argue otherwise. According to Smith et al. (2020), schools need training.
One study noted that "students who feel safe at school are more likely to engage in learning" (Garcia, 2021). The National Association of Social Workers (2021) sets standards. Kim (2015) is cited here too. It is important to note that this multifaceted issue plays a crucial role in the tapestry of education.
